The Activity page lists every change: who made it, when, and how (in the app, through the assistant, or through a connected tool).

## The record can't be edited
Activity entries can't be changed or deleted, by anyone.

## Assistant traces
For changes made through the assistant, the Activity entry links to the trace: the request, the steps the assistant took, the checks that ran, and who approved it.
