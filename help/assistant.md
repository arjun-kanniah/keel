The assistant (Keel) answers how-to questions from this help center and can prepare changes for you.

## What it can do
- Answer questions about Ratebook, with links to the help pages it used.
- Look up items, profiles, modules and recent activity.
- Prepare changes: single rates, bulk changes, profiles, modules and new items.

## Every change is a preview
The assistant never changes anything by itself. It shows a preview of exactly what would change, and you approve or cancel it. You can undo an approved change for 24 hours.

## What it can't do
It can't delete folders or items, export data, or change billing details. Those stay manual on purpose.

## Where its actions show up
Changes you approve from the assistant appear on the Activity page as "via Assistant", with a link to the full record of what the assistant did (its trace).
