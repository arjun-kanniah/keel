title: Changing many rates at once
version: 2026-11

Bulk changes raise or lower every rate in a folder (or every item of one type in a folder) by a percentage.

## Preview first
Every bulk change is shown as a preview first: each item with its old and new rate. Nothing changes until you approve the preview. Previews expire after 30 minutes.

## Rounding
New rates are rounded to the nearest cent.

## Large changes need an Owner
A change of more than 20% in either direction must be approved by an Owner. Admins can still prepare the change and send it to an Owner, who approves it from the Changes page.

## Limits
One change can touch at most 50 records. Rates must stay between $1 and $10,000. Split bigger jobs into smaller ones, for example one folder at a time.

## If someone else edits first
If any item in the preview is edited by someone else before you approve, the change is not applied and nothing is written. Ask again to get a fresh preview with the current rates.
