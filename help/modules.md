Modules switch features on and off for the whole company.

## Available modules
- Online booking: customers book visits from your website.
- Customer portal: customers see quotes, invoices and visit history.
- Deposits: collect a deposit when a quote is accepted.
- SMS reminders: text customers the day before a visit.
- QuickBooks sync: send invoices and payments to QuickBooks.

## Who can switch modules
Owners and Admins can switch most modules. QuickBooks sync touches billing, so only an Owner can switch it.

## Deposits
When Deposits is on, a deposit is requested when a customer accepts a quote. The deposit amount is set per quote.
