A pricing profile multiplies the rate of every item it is applied to. Standard (1×) is the default. Common extra profiles are After-hours (1.5×), Weekend (1.25×) and Holiday (2×).

## Add after-hours pricing
1. Create a profile, for example After-hours at 1.5×.
2. Apply it to the items it should cover, such as every Call-out item.
The base rates stay the same; the profile shows the multiplied price next to each item.

## Apply a profile to items
You can apply a profile to a whole folder, to every item of one type (in all folders or one), or to chosen items. Each item has exactly one profile.

## Multipliers
Multipliers must be between 0.5× and 3×.

## Remove a profile
Move its items back to Standard first; a profile that is still in use can't be removed.
