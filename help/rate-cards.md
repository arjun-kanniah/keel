Rate cards hold the prices you charge. They are grouped into folders, usually one per line of business (for example Residential HVAC, Commercial HVAC and Plumbing).

## Items
Each item has a name, a type (Maintenance, Call-out, Labor or Parts kit), a base rate, a unit (per visit, per hour, each, per job, per year) and a pricing profile. The price a customer pays is the base rate times the profile's multiplier.

## Change one rate
Open Rate cards, pick the folder, and click the rate you want to change. Type the new amount and press Enter. The change is saved straight away and shows on the Activity page.

## Notes on items
An item can carry a short note for your team, such as where a price came from. Notes are internal: customers never see them.

## Add an item
Ask the assistant to add it, or use Add item at the bottom of a folder. You need a name, a type, a rate and a unit. New items start on the Standard profile.
