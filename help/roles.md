Everyone in Ratebook has one of three roles.

## Owner
Can do everything, including billing settings (QuickBooks sync) and approving changes of more than 20%.

## Admin
Can change rates, profiles and modules (except billing ones), and prepare large changes for an Owner to approve.

## Viewer
Can see rate cards, profiles, modules and activity, but can't change anything.

## The assistant has your permissions, never more
The assistant acts as you. It can only do what your role allows, and every change it prepares still needs your approval.
