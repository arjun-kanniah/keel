You can undo any change made through a preview for 24 hours after it's applied, from the Changes page or from the assistant panel. After 24 hours, make a new change instead.

## How undo works
Undo is available for 24 hours after a change is applied. It puts back exactly the values that were there before. It works only if nothing it touches has been changed again since. If it has, undo stops and changes nothing, so it never overwrites newer work.

## Who can undo
Anyone whose role could have made the change can undo it.
